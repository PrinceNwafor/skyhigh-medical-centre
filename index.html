Why SkyHigh

A Higher Standard of Care,
Close to Home

Since 2007, SkyHigh Medical Centre has grown from a neighbourhood clinic into one of Magodo's most trusted 24-hour hospitals — combining modern equipment with doctors who genuinely listen.

From safe deliveries in the early hours to emergency surgery, paediatric care and our ICU & dialysis support, everything your family needs sits under one roof on Adekunle Banjo Avenue. No long referrals across Lagos — just clear, kind, dependable care.
