Our Story

A Clinic Built Around You

SkyHigh Medical Centre opened its doors in 2007 with a small, determined team and a belief that families in Magodo deserved more time, more clarity, and more compassion than the typical rushed appointment allowed.

Today we are a 24-hour multi-specialty centre on Adekunle Banjo Avenue — with an Emergency unit, Intensive Care, a Dialysis Centre, maternity, theatre and laboratory all under one roof. Yet that founding belief hasn't changed. Every decision, from how we schedule to how we welcome you at reception, starts with one question: what's best for the patient?

We invest in modern equipment and a caring, well-trained team not for show, but so our doctors can spend less time on paperwork and more time with you — day or night.
